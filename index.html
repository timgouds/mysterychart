Mystery Chart

The chart is real.
Its title is blacked out.

Five charts a day, all built from live public data. Everything is there except the one label that tells you what you're looking at.

  1. Read the evidence. Country names, years and numbers all stay visible. Only the subject is hidden, so the answer is there to be worked out rather than guessed.
  2. Name what it shows. Four options. One is right, and the wrong ones are chosen to be tempting.
  3. Wrong answers buy hints. Each one unlocks a clue, starting cryptic and ending obvious, and costing you points.
  4. Score out of 500. 100 for a chart you name straight away, 60 after one hint, 30 after two. All five first time earns a bonus. There's no clock. This rewards reading carefully, not clicking fast.
